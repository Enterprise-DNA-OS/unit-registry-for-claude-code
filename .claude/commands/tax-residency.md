description: "Tax status of every current holder: TFN quoted, NZ prescribed investor rate, non-resident withholding rate, CRS self-certification."
---

Read `CLAUDE.md` first. Run `npm run registry -- tax-residency`. Flag: AU residents with no TFN or ABN quoted (47% withheld at the next distribution), NZ PIE investors with no PIR (28% default), non-residents with no self-certification or no withholding rate. The withholding rate for a non-resident comes from the fund accountant: ask for it, then set it on the holder. This system does not give tax advice and does not lodge anything.
